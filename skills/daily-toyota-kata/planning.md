# Daily and weekly planning

- **Daily notes:** `journal/YYYY/MM-Month/YYYY-MM-DD.md` from `templates/journal.md`. Meetings not tied to an obstacle (weekly progress meetings, generic management meetings) go here.
- **Weekly updates:** Shared once a week with stakeholders.
	- **On the last day of the work week:** generate a file `journal/Weekly Update/WU YYYY-MM-DD.md`, covering all challenges.
- **Weekly update structure:** add an executive summary with risks at the top, then three sections:
	1. **Done:** main accomplishments since the previous update in the same series, collected from daily notes, obstacle logs, and achieved targets.
	2. **Next:** the prediction of what will be done by the next update.
	3. **Future:** longer-term goals and why they matter.
